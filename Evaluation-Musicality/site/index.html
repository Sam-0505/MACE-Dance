How to read the videos

Beat clock

Top right of each dancer. Each dot is a movement accent, placed by where it falls inside the musical beat (top = on the beat). The arrow is their average: the longer it is, the steadier the timing.

BPL · beat phase locking

Under the clock. How consistently the beat-rate part of the motion keeps one phase relative to the music beat over the whole clip, 0 to 1. It rewards a steady relation, not only hitting exactly on the beat.

Intensity graphs

Under each dancer. Motion intensity (QoM) is the dancer's smoothed motion energy; audio intensity (RMS) is the loudness of the song. Both cover the whole clip; the red line marks the current moment.

IMC · intensity correlation

Above the motion graph. Correlation between the two curves, −1 to 1: higher means the dance gets stronger when the music gets louder.
