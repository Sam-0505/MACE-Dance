What the videos show

  • Beat clock (top right of each dancer): each dot is a movement accent, placed by where it falls inside the musical beat (top = on the beat). The arrow is their average; a longer arrow means the dancer hits the beat at a steadier point.
  • BPL (beat phase locking, under the clock): how consistently the beat-rate part of the motion keeps one phase relative to the music beat over the whole clip (0 to 1, higher = more locked). It does not require hitting exactly on the beat, only a steady relation.
  • Motion intensity (QoM) and Audio intensity (RMS) (graphs under each dancer): the smoothed motion energy of the dancer and the loudness of the song, over the whole clip; the red line is the current moment.
  • IMC (next to the motion graph): correlation between the two curves (−1 to 1, higher = the dance gets stronger when the music gets louder).

Keys: Space play / pause, ← → 2 s back / forward. Real Dance is the motion-capture dance recorded for the song.
